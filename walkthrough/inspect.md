# Read a photo without leaving VS Code

- Ctrl or Cmd + mouse wheel zooms, and so do the + and - buttons and the keys + and -. The key 0 fits the window, 1 shows 1 image pixel per screen pixel.
- The bottom line shows x, y and the RGB value under the cursor. Double-click copies the hex value.
- Camera, lens, exposure, ISO and the time the photo was taken are in the panel on the right.
- A GPS position shows as latitude and longitude numbers. Copy coordinates puts them on the clipboard. No map is loaded.
- Bursts, stereo pairs and other multi-image files get an Image picker. The left and right arrow keys step through the images.
